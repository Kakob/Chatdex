# Share Build Story — product requirements

Status: Proposed, 2026-09-08. [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) owns delivery; [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) owns behavior.

## Purpose and audience

Help the user show technical people how they explore a problem and build software
with AI. Readers may be collaborators or employers; the same story should help them
understand/evaluate the work and take something useful from it. Income opportunities
are a hoped-for long-term outcome, not a product metric promised by this feature.

A story may describe unfinished, unsatisfying or unresolved work. Its value can be a
method, example, question or precise account of failed approaches, not necessarily
finished code or a polished success narrative.

## Agreed experience

Begin a private draft while investigating. Browse decisions, chat excerpts, code,
specifications and experiment results and select Add to build story. Gather selected
material into an evidence tray beside a writing surface. Arrange moments around the
problem, choices, interventions, surprises and current state. These are optional
writing prompts, not a rigid form.

The user authors the narrative. AI can draft or reshape under their direction using
selected evidence: draft an opening, connect two moments, or emphasize unresolved
questions. Suggestions are separate until adopted; AI must not invent motivation,
author attribution, successful outcomes or certainty to make the story smoother.

Decision authoring and correction occur upstream in
[Decision Authoring & Evidence](../decision-authoring-evidence/PRD.md). Story prose and
annotations may qualify a selected revision but do not silently rewrite it. A published
story stands behind a selected interpretation while retaining honest AI/user origins.

## Reader experience

Open an unlisted Chatdex-hosted page without needing the author's private workspace.
Read the narrative, expand selected evidence with sufficient surrounding context, and
use approved reusable material. Link to the author's chosen portfolio/contact channels.
No comments, accounts for readers, community feed or collaborative editing in v1.

The reader may skim the insight, inspect how it was reached, or go directly to a
reusable procedure/checklist/example. Reusable material is optional; honest uncertainty
can itself be useful. Progressive disclosure stops at the approved shared boundary.
It never reveals a full private conversation or repository because a reader clicks deeper.

## Review and publication

Review the narrative, evidence excerpts, filenames, links and downloads together.
Sanitize credentials, personal information and irrelevant private material without
making the edited evidence misleading. Redaction should preserve meaning or clearly
mark an excerpt as incomplete; it cannot claim the source proves something it no
longer supports. Automated suggestions assist but do not certify safety.

Publish a reviewed snapshot to an unlisted hosted URL by default. Anyone with the
link may read or forward it; unlisted is not restricted access. No public directory.
Private draft edits remain private until explicit publication of an updated snapshot.
Unpublish withdraws the hosted page. Previously downloaded/exported copies cannot be
recalled. Provide export as well as hosted publication using the same reviewed content.

## Analytics

Provide aggregate activity: page opens, approved evidence expansions, reusable-material
or export downloads, and contact-link clicks. Explain counts as activity signals, not
proof of reading, understanding, unique people or interest in hiring. No per-reader
identity, anonymous journey replay, fingerprinting or individual profiles in v1.
Analytics are visible to the author, not other readers. The content remains usable
when analytics delivery is blocked or fails.

## Shared first story and success

“How Chatdex gets code from GitHub and uses it as evidence” follows an investigation
of implicit choices and the exploration of future agent execution. Collect material
while it is unfinished. Show the user's framing, AI suggestions, corrections, selected
experiments, limitations and next questions. No requirement to finish agent execution
before publishing. [Experiment Lab](../experiment-lab/PRD.md) supplies selected attempts.

A reader can explain the contribution, inspect its evidence and identify something
applicable to their own work. The author can verify what crossed the sharing boundary,
update/unpublish deliberately, and see modest aggregate engagement signals.
